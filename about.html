Emeka Gold

Lead Consultant · EMGHUB

Emeka Gold is a Research & Data Analysis Consultant and the Lead Consultant at EMGHUB. He supports students, researchers and organisations with practical research guidance, data analysis and academic consultancy.

His work brings together structured research design, careful analysis and clear interpretation of findings. Through EMGHUB, he aims to help clients make sense of research questions, communicate evidence effectively and approach academic work with care and integrity.
